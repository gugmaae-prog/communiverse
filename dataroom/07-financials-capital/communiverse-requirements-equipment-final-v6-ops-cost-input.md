## Sheet: Open questions

| # | Question | Answer (latest Q&A form, v17) |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Describe the ideal Communiverse ambassador. Who should we never sign? | The ideal ambassador is curious / warm / rooted in their city / committed / honest / comfortable but subtle on camera / understands handmade work / has a small but engaged audience. They share Communiverse's values and are genuinely into connecting artisans to the world and the right community. They do not need to be polished on camera. Communiverse wants to capture real moments, so the camera presence should stay subtle. We never sign someone who pressures artisans or promises what we can't deliver. |
| Q2 | What is the biggest risk in the first 30 days, and how will you handle it? | I don't see a major risk. The main thing in the first 30 days is my own learning of the role. In the first 30 days I will make sure each ambassador's content matches the brand / streamline content so it complements the Communiverse brand guidelines / identify each ambassador's strengths and assign their key needs / leverage their strengths to advance their goals. |
| Q3 | Which metrics will you use in month one? | Engagement / artisans signed / footage delivered on time and to standard. Cost per signed artisan, both raw and market-adjusted (the artisan acquisition cost), per ambassador. Profitability and fairness test: it tracks each ambassador's pace against other ambassadors' performance and payment. This ensures no ambassador is over- or under-rewarded relative to their output and local market costs. A CRM is needed to measure timely delivery. monday.com is one option off the top of my head, but I prefer something more custom. I will discuss with the team which CRM is best. |
| Q4 | How will you recruit ambassadors? | Start with warm intros from Luna and Hassan: two or three names each, in their city, who already film or post about local makers. Post in artisan workshops, where the audience already gathers. Run one open call per city with a simple one-minute video prompt, to see how candidates come across on camera before discussing rates. Do workshop visits first to find people who already live this and to gather content. Move to mock-ups once there is a shortlist. |
| Q5 | How will ambassadors be paid? | Ambassadors are not paid a monthly salary, retainer or fixed fee. Compensation is based solely on the sales of the artisans they recruit. No per-artisan fee amount is set in this form. Under consideration, not confirmed: ambassadors who become really comfortable with the programme may be offered shares in the regional SPV as an additional long-term incentive, on top of the sales-based pay. Ambassador compensation (the sales-based pay and any possible SPV shares) is strictly internal. Ambassadors must not tell artisans that they receive money from the artisan's sales. It is how Communiverse compensates the ambassador and is not disclosed to artisans. Travel and other support for ambassadors is not pay. It is controlled case by case, depending on need, and must be specific. |
| Q6 | What happens when an ambassador underperforms? | A performance improvement plan with coaching / SMART goals (Specific / Measurable / Achievable / Relevant / Time-bound) / 30-day tracking. Tracking is automated through a scorecard dashboard. Coaching uses the sandwich approach: agree what went well / what needs improvement / what could be done better. |
| Q7 | How will you keep ambassadors engaged and retain them? | Monthly showcase or reward for emerging leaders / top points system / leaderboard / rotating exclusives. Google Local Guides style points that earn better destinations. |
| Q8 | How will you handle brand drift and protect artisans? | Coaching, with a reference card for every ambassador. Coach first and explain why. No exploitation of artisans. They give consent and receive fair credit, backed by a signed consent or a simple written artisan agreement. |
| Q9 | What equipment do ambassadors need? | The standard kit for everyone is the DJI Osmo Nano (about 1,281 AED) plus a tripod (55 to 184 AED). That is about 1,468 AED per ambassador. Meta glasses are out of the standard kit because they are restricted in some countries. They may be considered later as a sponsored item. Idea: approach a tech partner for sponsorship. Luna needs training with me. |
| Q10 | How will you recruit in new cities? | Study the output generated by current ambassadors first, then apply those learnings to target other cities. |
| Q11 | What is your approach to being on camera and to the artisans? | Never exploitative / authentic / organic / honest. The focus is on creative bidding and VR content, and on how things are made. Authenticity is key. |
| Q12 | How many hours a week can you commit? | 25 hours a week, in 3 to 4 focused hours each day. |
| Q13 | What are your blockers? | The learning curve is the main blocker. What I need to learn: the ambassador programme and onboarding process / the CRM and scorecard dashboard for tracking delivery and performance / Communiverse's brand guidelines / how to coach ambassadors, including training them on video and storytelling. |
| Q14 | Is anything unclear in the brief? | Nothing unclear for now. |
