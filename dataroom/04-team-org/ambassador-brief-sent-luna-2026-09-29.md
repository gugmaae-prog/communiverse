# Communiverse in brief

Communiverse connects culture, commerce and community. We sign and verify artisans in writing, then give people four ways to buy from them:

- Learn: workshops and corporate sessions with artisans.

- Make: kits, made goods and corporate gifting.

- Collect: a marketplace for tangible items: finished pieces, one of one art and commissioned work.

- Belong: the Craft Journey (subscription), a longer relationship with craft and the people behind it.

Our first two regions are the UAE and Colombia. Our purpose is to keep crafts alive by giving artisans real income, real customers and a wider audience.

# What an ambassador is, and what we offer

Ambassadors represent their city/country. They travel through it, find and bring in artisans, and tell those artisans' stories: preserving crafts, celebrating local masters, and showing the human side of handmade work.

- You travel and vlog. As you move through your city/country looking for artisans, you vlog the journey: the search, the places, the people and the crafts you discover. The vlog is a core part of the job, not an extra.

- You onboard artisans and stay with them. You bring artisans in through our official onboarding process, help them create content, and keep in regular contact after they join.

- A community, not a list of influencers. You are connected with the artisans you bring on and with other ambassadors, as one network for arts, culture, curation and community.

- You film, we edit. You capture raw footage to Communiverse standards; our in-house team edits it in a style that reflects your culture, within the Communiverse brand.

- Quality over quantity. We want several verified artisans in each craft, not one gatekeeper per craft, and every one must meet our quality bar.

What Communiverse offers you

- Exposure through Communiverse channels and our wider network.

- Expense paid domestic travel when on assignment.

- Equipment where needed: a mini action camera if your own camera does not meet our standards.

- The chance to do real good for craft communities.

- Your compensation is linked to the artisans you bring on, as set out in your ambassador agreement. That is why staying in good contact with them matters.

# Your responsibilities

## 1. Represent Communiverse

- Be our voice and face in your city/country, online and in person.

- Speak about Communiverse accurately, warmly and without craft jargon.

- Uphold our standards of conduct with artisans, customers and partners.

## 2. Travel, vlog and find artisans

- Travel through your city/country to discover skilled artisans and emerging artists across a range of crafts.

- Vlog the journey as you go, to Communiverse footage standards.

- Understand the quality we look for, and only bring on artisans who meet it.

## 3. Onboard artisans

- Onboard every artisan through the official Communiverse onboarding process. Keiffer designs it, Haseeb approves it and Elferah manages it.

- Support artisans through verification, signing and setting up their profile.

## 4. Help artisans create content

- Film with each artisan: their hands at work, their space, their pieces and their story.

- Direct them on camera so the footage looks natural and meets our standards.

- Leave them with Communiverse content guidelines, and show them how to use them, so they can keep creating good content when you are not there.

## 5. Maintain the relationship

- Stay in regular, respectful contact with every artisan you bring on. Your compensation is linked to them, so good communication is key.

- Your communication with artisans is monitored in RAL, our internal operations system.

- RAL sends artisans reminders to complete their profiles and update their content. When an artisan needs help, you will be asked to assist.

## 6. Build the community

- Connect artisans with each other and with other ambassadors.

- Attend or host Communiverse sessions and events when asked.

- Share local insight: what people want, which crafts are rising, which are at risk.

## 7. Stay accountable

- Agree monthly goals with Keiffer and share progress with Elferah.

- Raise problems early, and ask for support when you need it.

# From discovery to an active artisan: the steps

- Discover: find an artisan on your travels whose work meets our quality bar, and capture the moment in your vlog.

- Connect: meet them, explain Communiverse simply, and check their interest.

- Log: add them to the artisan pipeline so operations can see them.

- Onboard: take them through the official onboarding process.

- Verify: our team verifies the artisan in person; you support where helpful.

- Sign: the artisan signs with Communiverse.

- Create: film their story with them, and leave them with our content guidelines.

- Stay in touch: keep regular contact, and help when RAL flags that they need support.

Never promise an artisan pay, bookings or exclusivity. Only the Communiverse team can agree terms.

# Footage standards and workflow

Communiverse has set video footage standards, and every vlog and artisan video must meet them. Keiffer shares the full standard with sample videos, style references and shot lists.

Equipment

- If your camera does not meet our standard, Communiverse will provide a mini action camera with the stabilisation needed for smooth, good looking footage.

Skills you need

- Lighting: using natural and available light well, and avoiding harsh shadows and backlight.

- Camera: settings, framing, movement and when to use camera effects.

- Direction: guiding artisans on camera so they look natural and the story is clear.

Footage basics

- Picture: stable, well lit, in focus, at the resolution and orientation Keiffer specifies.

- Sound: clear voice and low background noise; use a microphone for interviews.

- Two kinds of content: your travel vlog (the journey and the search) and artisan stories (the artisan, their hands at work, their space, their pieces and why the craft matters to them).

- Consent: get permission before filming, and never film customers or children without consent.

- Delivery: raw files uploaded to the shared folder, labelled with the artisan's name, craft, city/country and date.

- Posting: do not publish Communiverse content yourself before it has been edited and approved, unless Keiffer agrees.

Workflow: you film, Keiffer reviews the raw footage, Hassan's in-house editor edits it, and Hassan publishes it on Communiverse channels.
