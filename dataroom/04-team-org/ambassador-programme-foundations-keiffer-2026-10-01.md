Ambassador Programme: Foundations

Keiffer Japeth / Marketing, GTM and Ambassador Programme Lead
For Haseeb Wasim / Elferah Saidal
1 October 2026

These are the foundations needed to build the Communiverse ambassador programme from the ground up. Each item has a short description of what it is and why it is needed. Amounts are in AED. Items marked pending depend on team decisions.

Section 1: Business foundations

1. Programme framework and scorecard

The rules of the programme: roles, the ambassador journey from recruitment to onboarding to reviews, and the scorecard. Month-one scorecard measures: engagement / artisans signed / footage on time and to standard / cost per signed artisan (raw and market-adjusted) / profitability and fairness test. Underperformance follows a performance improvement plan with SMART goals, 30-day tracking and sandwich-style coaching.

2. Pay and incentive model

Currently sales-based only: about 551 AED per signed and verified artisan, with no salary, retainer or fixed fee. Travel and support are case by case and specific. Regional SPV shares are under consideration, not confirmed. Compensation is internal and not disclosed to artisans. Pending team confirmation after the team discussion.

3. Artisan consent and agreement templates

A signed consent or a simple written artisan agreement, covering consent to film and share, fair credit, and what happens to the content. This protects the artisans and the brand, and it is the base for the no-exploitation rule. Needs a review for the UAE and Colombia.

4. Brand style guide with samples

Communiverse's brand guidelines for ambassador content, with sample posts and clips of what good looks like (authentic / organic / honest, subtle on camera, focused on how things are made). Includes a one-page reference card for every ambassador.

5. Recruitment plan per city

Start with warm intros from Luna and Hassan, post in artisan workshops, run one open call per city with a one-minute video prompt, and visit workshops first before mock-ups once there is a shortlist. For new cities, study the output from current ambassadors first, then apply the learnings. Still needed: a target list of cities and a shortlist process.

6. Budget and cost model

The travel package model for the UAE and Colombia first, with India and Egypt prepared: flights by season, modest hotels at local rates, transport, groceries and per-trip totals. Standard kit about 1,468 AED per ambassador (DJI Osmo Nano plus tripod). Sales-based pay of 551 AED per signed artisan. Still needed: a cap per trip and who books travel.

7. Reporting cadence to Haseeb and Elferah

A fixed rhythm for updates, for example a short weekly update to Elferah on deadlines and a monthly review with Elferah and Haseeb on the scorecard, spend and decisions. Haseeb approves pay, agreements and the onboarding process. The exact cadence is to be agreed.

8. Legal and compliance basics for UAE and Colombia

Check with a local adviser before scaling: how ambassadors are engaged (contractor or not, with no salary), consent and image rights for artisans, data protection and privacy for recordings, advertising and endorsement disclosure rules, and rules on filming in each place. Also confirm how the artisan-facing wording fits the consent and fair credit commitment. This list is a starting point for an adviser, not legal advice.

Section 2: Technical foundations

1. CRM

Tracks artisans, ambassadors and content in one place, and measures timely delivery. monday.com is one option from the top of my head, but a more custom setup is preferred. The team will decide which CRM is best.

2. Content management

A shared home for the style guide, reference cards and approved sample content, with version control so ambassadors always use the current version.

3. Dashboard

Automates the scorecard metrics and flags underperformance, for example when the 30-day tracking falls behind. It pulls from the CRM and analytics.

4. Consent and agreement storage

Secure storage for signed consents and artisan agreements, linked to each artisan and ambassador record, with retrieval when asked.

5. Communication channels

A community chat for ambassadors, for announcements / questions / sharing wins / the monthly showcase. Plus one place for updates to Haseeb and Elferah.

6. Basic analytics

Reach / engagement / bookings / sales, per ambassador and per city, so the cost per signed artisan and the profitability and fairness test can be calculated.

Suggested order of work

First: pay model decision / consent and agreement templates / brand style guide and reference card / CRM choice.

Next: scorecard and dashboard / reporting cadence / recruitment per city.

Then: legal check for the UAE and Colombia before the first ambassador is onboarded, and analytics and community chat set up in parallel.
