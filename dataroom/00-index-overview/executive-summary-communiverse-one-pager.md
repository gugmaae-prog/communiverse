# Communiverse: Executive Summary

Pre-seed · Prepared for investment-readiness assessment (Amaya IDI, ADI and Clara) · 15 Oct 2026, updated 15 Oct (pre-seed framing) · This is a readiness summary, not an offer or a sales document: it separates what we have built (with evidence) from what we are building (roadmap targets). Every figure is traceable in the Key Figures sheet (CURRENT, 1g6tSJ8c…) [KF-xx]; anything without a source is tagged UNVERIFIED.

What we are building. A marketplace designed around verified artisans (verification protocol not yet written; 0 artisans verified or signed today [KF-20]). Each artisan earns through five lines from one relationship: workshops and experiences, corporate programmes, commissions and brand collaborations, kits and gifts, and regional marketplace sales. Artisans never pay to join (deck s9). It starts in Dubai/UAE (demand) and Colombia (supply, selling to US buyers), and grows city by city through local ambassadors.

Context (sourced). People place 62% less value on art once it's labelled AI-made [KF-60]. 82% of Colombian craft households earn below minimum wage [KF-59]. Dubai had 18.72m international visitors in 2024 [KF-50], and 66% of them visited cultural or heritage sites [KF-51].
