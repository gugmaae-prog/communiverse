## Summary

Global markets are context, not TAM. The deck's three totals are separate markets and shouldn't be added together.

SAM is built bottom-up from Dubai. Dubai alone has about 12.36m culturally engaged international visitors a year (calc) and 4.58m residents [KF-50, KF-51, KF-52]. One craft session costs about US$57–60 at public Dubai prices [KF-56].

SOM has no sourced inputs yet. It's a visible formula, and every input is a GAP until the financial model exists. We show one illustrative capacity check, labelled as such.

## 4. Supply corridor: Colombia → US

About 350,000 Colombian artisans, 15% of manufacturing jobs [KF-57]. US$19 per artisan, calc), with the US the #1 buyer [KF-58]. 82% of craft-income households earn below minimum wage [KF-59].

US buyer demand for Colombian craft is GAP: it needs a sourced US import or consumer figure. Until then this is a supply-side thesis, not a sized market.
