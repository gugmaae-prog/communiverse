Quiet Awakenings.
An evening of poetry.

A Saturday evening in Al Quoz with Avid Learning Middle East, four poets, and a room full of listeners.

We were there
Keiffer ↗ Luna ↗

Keiffer and Luna joined Quiet Awakenings at the Avid Centre for Culture and Creative Expressions in Dubai. The evening brought together Adel Awad, Tasveer Maharaj, Arpita John and Andrew Riad to share their poetry.

Each poet brought a different perspective. In its recap, Avid reflects on how their words and performances offered a way into each other’s experiences.

Our photos catch the moments around the readings, too: printed pages in hand, people gathered in the gallery, and a pause to look at the art on the walls. Keiffer and Luna were there as part of the Communiverse circle.

Thank you to Avid Learning and the poets for the evening. Here are a few moments we brought back.
